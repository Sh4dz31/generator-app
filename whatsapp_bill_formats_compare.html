1 · Formatted text cheapest
Plain text with *bold* and an auto-linked pay URL. One text send, no media.

My read: Option 2 (template + Pay button) is the realistic default — it's what subscribers will tap to pay, it's cheap enough, and the body text carries the legal LBP breakdown. Option 3 is the upgrade if operators say the plain look feels cheap (you'd render the receipt server-side as an image — that's where the nice card you liked actually lives in production). Option 1 is the fallback when cost matters most. All three keep the bill LBP-primary and the Whish amount in USD. Shown in Arabic here; the English equivalent renders identically.
